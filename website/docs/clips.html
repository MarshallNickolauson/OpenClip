Clips & timeline

Preview

Every suggestion plays in a real 9:16 player. Arrow through the list with ↑ ↓ (or K J) and the next clip autoplays. Press L to loop one, Q to restart it.

Choose

Press X or Return to tick a clip for export. Press ⌫ to dismiss one you do not want.

Trim

Each clip has its own timeline showing the waveform, the spoken words, caption blocks and the framing path.

  • Drag the edges to trim. They snap to word boundaries.
  • Step by sentence, or press [ and ] to set the in and out points.
  • Step by 0.5 s with ← → (hold ⇧ for 5 s).
  • Press N to create a new clip yourself.
  • Hold ⌘ and scroll to zoom the timeline.

Undo and saving

Undo and redo with ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z. Edits stay in memory until you save with ⌘S. A * after the project name means unsaved changes, and closing asks first.
