Cookie Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This website sets no cookies, so there is no cookie banner.

What is stored in your browser

If you change the theme (light or dark), the site remembers your choice in your browser's local storage under the key oc-theme. This is a preference you asked for, it stays on your device, and it is never sent to us or anyone else. Clearing your site data removes it.

Analytics

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. It does not add its own analytics or tracking scripts. See the privacy policy.

The OpenClip desktop app does not use cookies.
