Long talks in, vertical clips out.

Drop in a 30-minute sermon, talk or podcast. OpenClip transcribes it on your Mac, finds the moments that stand on their own, follows the speaker's face, burns in captions and exports 9:16 clips. Free, open source, and nothing is uploaded.

Wide in, tall out.

Just 3 Steps.

  1. Drop

    Drop a video in. It is transcribed and faces are found, on your Mac.

  2. Pick

    Claude, or the built-in Quick pick, suggests 5 to 10 moments. Arrow through them in a real 9:16 player.

  3. Export

    Tick the clips you like and export them all in one batch.

Suggested clips, ready to play

Every suggestion opens in a real vertical player and autoplays as you arrow through the list. Tick the keepers, dismiss the rest, and trim any clip on its own timeline with waveform, words and word-snapping edges.

A camera that follows the face

The virtual camera holds still while the speaker stays centred, glides when they move, and cuts when your source cuts between cameras. Choose who to follow, set the zoom, nudge it, or drop keyframes by dragging the preview.

Captions burned in, made on your Mac

Transcription runs on whisper.cpp with the Metal GPU. Pick the Pop, Boxed or Clean look, highlight the spoken word, set size, position and colour, and fix a word by double-clicking it.

Batch export, hardware encoded

1080×1920 or 720×1280, H.264 or HEVC with AAC audio, up to 30 fps. Encoding goes through the Mac's own AVFoundation and VideoToolbox, so there is no bundled codec to license.

Claude picks the moments

Local MCP server
openclip-mcp ships inside the app. Claude Desktop or Claude Code launches it on your Mac over stdio. Nothing is hosted, and there is no account or API key.
Cheap on tokens
Claude reads about twenty pre-ranked, sentence-aligned candidates instead of the whole transcript: a few thousand tokens regardless of video length, in two calls.
Clips appear live
Claude saves clips by sentence id and they show up in the app as it works. No Claude? Quick pick scores the moments locally.

The rest of the app

Jump cuts
One toggle removes the silences between sentences, in the preview and in the export.
Clip timeline
Waveform, words, caption blocks and the framing path. Drag edges with word snapping, step by sentence, set in and out.
Choose a model
Fast (base.en), Balanced (small.en) or Best (large-v3-turbo). Downloaded once, only when you ask.
Native app
Rust and egui. No web view, no Electron. Captions are drawn with Core Image and CoreText.
Plain project folders
Projects live in ~/OpenClip Projects/ and exports go to ~/Movies/OpenClip. Edits stay in memory until you save.
Undo everywhere
Undo and redo across trims, captions and framing, plus an unsaved-changes marker.

Fast enough to stay in flow

Import
A 75-minute, 2 GB 720p sermon was transcribed and its faces found in about 8.5 minutes. Transcription ran near 9× realtime on small.en.
Export
A 32-second clip exported in about 12 seconds, roughly 2 to 3× realtime.

Measured on an M-series Mac. Your numbers depend on the chip, the model and the source video.

Quick keys in the editor

  • Space Play and pause.
  • ↑ ↓ Previous or next clip.
  • X Return Tick the clip for export.
  • [ ] Set the clip's in and out points.
  • T Toggle jump cuts.
  • ⌘ E Export.
